The problem

AA normal 3D printer can only make things smaller than itself, and it's slow. Robots that move around while they print don't have that limit. The hard part is that they need to know exactly where they are, down to about a millimeter, and cheap ways of tracking a robot are only good to about a centimeter.

What we did

The lab's research combined cameras and laser sensors to help each robot work out its position more precisely. I worked on the robots and the software we used to test how accurate they were.

By the end of the project, the robots printed a floor plan at least 3 meters by 3 meters.

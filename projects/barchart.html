What it is

IIn the summer of 2020 I was a STEP intern at Google. My team ran a service that sends configuration changes out to running software, a few groups at a time.

Engineers wanted two answers at a glance: did each stage of a rollout start and finish when it should, and is the rollout happening now slower or faster than usual?

What I built

I built an interactive bar chart of past rollouts. You can slide along the timeline to choose which rollouts to look at, show only the finished ones, and hover over a bar to see when it started and how it ended.

A small summary next to the chart shows the usual range, so you can tell at a glance whether the rollout in front of you is taking longer than normal.

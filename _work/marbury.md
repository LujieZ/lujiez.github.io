## Context

<span class="dropcap" aria-hidden="true">I</span><span class="sr-only">I</span>n litigation, both sides collect, process, and review large sets of documents. This is called eDiscovery. The work is slow and expensive, and legal teams are careful about handing any of it to AI they can't inspect.

## My role

I co-founded Marbury and built the platform from the first commit. I lead development.

## The constraint

Lawyers have to be able to explain every decision about a document. So anything the AI does needs a record that a person can review.

> Anything the AI does needs a record that a person can review.

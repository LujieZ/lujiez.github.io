At Google Cloud

Identity for agents

I've been at Google since 2022, on Cloud Security. Most of my work is about how software proves who it is.

Lately that software is AI agents. I work on letting agents use the tools they need without ever holding the keys themselves, and on making a stolen credential useless to whoever took it. I also built the part of Google Cloud where Gemini suggests what access people should have.

At Marbury AI

An eDiscovery platform, from the first commit

I co-founded Marbury in 2024 and I'm the CTO. We help legal teams process and review documents for litigation without giving up control of the process.

I built the platform from the start. It reads in a case's documents, lets lawyers search them by exact words or by meaning, and has an AI reviewer that writes down the reason for every call it makes.

About the author

A little more about me

II moved from China to the U.S. for high school when I was sixteen, then went to NYU as the first in my family to go to college. I studied Computer Science there (B.S. 2021, magna cum laude) with minors in math and finance.

At Google I'm also on call for some of Google Cloud's most critical systems, and I've advised teams across Google Cloud, and at Waymo, on how their software should handle identity. I've mentored engineers, helped an intern convert to full-time, and organized a 500-person event as a Women@ ambassador.

From 2023 to 2024 I also led engineering part-time at Saturday Inc, building a coach portal for team orders, seat assignments, and subscription payments.

Outside work I paint and work out.

AAt Google Cloud Security I work on identity: how AI agents and other software prove who they are, and what they're allowed to do. I'm also on call for some of Google Cloud's most critical systems, and I've advised teams across Google Cloud, and at Waymo, on how their software should handle identity.

Outside Google, I'm co-founder and CTO of Marbury AI. From 2023 to 2024 I led engineering part-time at Saturday Inc, building a coach portal for team orders, seat assignments, and subscription payments.

I studied Computer Science at NYU (B.S. 2021, magna cum laude) with minors in math and finance.
